Foundations for Application

An applied path through AI literacy.

AI Builder 100 is designed for professionals, students, and curious learners who want a clear framework for engaging with artificial intelligence. The course moves from foundational problem framing to responsible systems and AI workflow automation.

Each module builds on the last, connecting technical concepts to practical decisions, ethical awareness, and the human context in which AI operates.

What you will be able to do

01

Frame AI opportunities

Recognise where AI adds value, define a sound problem, and know when AI is not the right answer.

02

Read systems critically

Identify the data, models, feedback loops, and human decisions that shape real-world AI systems.

03

Apply responsible practice

Use practical tools for fairness, transparency, human oversight, and communicating limitations.

04

Design intelligent workflows

Understand how agents, tools, and orchestration patterns can support multi-step work.
