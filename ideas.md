## Idea 2: "Structured Intelligence" — Editorial Warm Modernism
**Design Movement:** Swiss International Typography + Warm Editorial

**Core Principles:**
- Off-white warm canvas with deep forest green and terracotta accents
- Strong typographic hierarchy using oversized numerals for module numbering
- Structured grid with intentional "breaks" — one element always bleeds outside its column
- Tactile quality: subtle paper texture, ink-like borders

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: warm cream (#FAF7F2) — approachable, human, not sterile
- Primary: Deep forest green (#1B4332) — trust, growth, expertise
- Accent: Terracotta (#C1440E) — energy, action, warmth
- Neutral: Warm gray (#8C7B6B)
- Emotional intent: credible expertise with human warmth

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Oversized module numbers (01–05) anchoring each section, bleeding into margins
- Hero: full-bleed left-aligned text block with an editorial pull-quote treatment
- Modules in a dense editorial card grid — varied card heights for rhythm
- Pricing as a "featured article" style callout

**Signature Elements:**
- Large oversized numerals as decorative anchors
- Thin 1px terracotta rule lines
- "Stamp" style badges for course metadata (duration, format, price)

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cards lift with a warm shadow on hover (no glow — organic feel)
- CTA uses a fill-from-bottom animation
- Module accordion with smooth height transitions

**Animation:**
- Sections fade up on scroll with 60ms stagger
- Number counters animate on viewport entry
- Smooth accordion for module details

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Playfair Display" Bold — editorial gravitas
- Body: "Source Sans 3" Regular — clean readability
- Numbers/Labels: "Space Grotesk" 700 — structural contrast

## Idea 3: "Signal & Clarity" — Bold Geometric Futurism
**Design Movement:** Constructivist Digital — bold geometry, asymmetric tension

**Core Principles:**
- Deep navy base with vivid electric blue and sharp white type
- Diagonal cuts and angled section dividers break the monotony of rectangular layouts
- Module cards use a "file tab" metaphor — stacked, labeled, interactive
- Strong left-aligned typographic spine throughout

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep navy (#0D1B2A) — depth and professionalism
- Primary: Electric blue (#0066FF) — digital, energetic, modern
- Accent: Lime green (#39FF14) — used only for key CTAs and progress indicators
- Text: Pure white on dark, near-black on light sections
- Emotional intent: bold confidence, digital-native, action-oriented

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Hero with diagonal clip-path dividing a dark navy left panel (text) from a vivid blue right panel (visual)
- Modules as horizontal "file tabs" — click to expand with smooth animation
- Pricing section uses a large card with a diagonal stripe accent
- Footer with a bold typographic statement

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal clip-path section transitions
- "File tab" module navigation
- Animated gradient border on the primary CTA button

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Tabs slide open horizontally with spring physics
- Hover on nav items draws an underline from left
- Scroll progress indicator along the left edge of the page

**Animation:**
- Hero: text slides in from left, visual fades in from right (200ms stagger)
- Module tabs: spring-based height expansion
- CTA: gradient border rotates on hover (CSS @keyframes)

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Barlow Condensed" ExtraBold 800 — bold, condensed impact
- Body: "DM Sans" Regular 400 — clean, modern readability
- Labels: "Barlow Condensed" 600 uppercase
